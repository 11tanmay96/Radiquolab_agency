Overview

"Fashion That Reflects You"

Bella Leathers is a handcrafted leather bag brand that previously had no online presence, selling purely through word of mouth and in-person channels. This project involved designing and building their first-ever e-commerce website from the ground up, giving the brand a digital storefront that reflects its artisanal identity while enabling online discovery and sales for the first time.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, E-commerce Web Development, Brand Storytelling
Problem Identification
No Digital Presence:

Bella Leathers had no website at all, meaning potential customers had no way to discover, browse, or purchase products online.

Unstructured Brand Story:

The brand's handmade origins, values, and craftsmanship existed only as word-of-mouth knowledge — with no place to present them to new customers.

No Online Sales Channel:

All sales happened offline, limiting reach and growth, and leaving revenue dependent on physical/local presence alone.

Building Trust from Zero:

As a new online entrant, the brand had no reviews, no visible catalog, and no established credibility signals to reassure first-time online buyers.

Solution

Built a Complete E-commerce Presence from Scratch:

Designed and developed the brand's first website, establishing an online sales channel where none existed before.

Trust-Building Value Bar:

With no online history to lean on, introduced a "What Makes Us Different" section paired with real offline credibility stats (1500+ Bags Crafted, 1000+ Happy Customers, 250+ 5-Star Reviews) to establish trust from day one.

Brand Storytelling Timeline:

Documented the brand's previously untold origin story through a hand-illustrated 2023–2025 "About Us" timeline, giving the brand a face and history for the first time.

Promotions & Social Proof:

Added a launch-ready offer zone (End Year Sale, New Arrival Sale, Black Friday Sale) and a testimonials section to surface existing offline customer goodwill and drive early conversions.
