Overview

"Connecting Farms to Global Markets with Organic Excellence."

BRG Global Trade is a website for an exporter of premium organic spices, pulses, and grains, built around the tagline "Connecting Farms to Global Markets with Organic Excellence." The site presents the company's sourcing process, product catalog (Turmeric, Chilli Powder, Pulses, Onion, Rice, Curcuma Longa), certifications, and client testimonials to build trust with international buyers such as hotels, retailers, and distributors.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Web Development, Content Strategy
Problem Identification
Low User Engagement:

The website lacked a clear, compelling structure to capture visitor attention and guide them toward taking action.

Difficult Navigation:

Important information such as products, certifications, and the sourcing process was hard to find, leading to a poor user experience.

Weak Lead Generation:

There was no strong, streamlined way for interested buyers to inquire or request quotes, resulting in missed business opportunities.

Lack of Trust Signals:

Limited visibility of certifications, process transparency, and client testimonials made it harder for international buyers to trust the brand.

Solution

Improved UI/UX Design:

Redesigned the interface to be clean, professional, and visually rooted in the agro-export industry using farm and shipping imagery alongside line-art illustrations to build brand identity.

Streamlined Information Architecture:

Organized products, process, and certifications into clear, digestible sections so buyers can quickly find what they need.

Optimized Lead Capture:

Added prominent "Get a Quote" and "Send Enquiry" CTAs throughout the site, including per-product quote buttons, to convert visitors into leads.

Enhanced Credibility Section:

Introduced a "Why Choose Us" section, a 4-step process breakdown (Sourcing → Quality Check → Packaging → Global Shipping), certifications badges, and client testimonials to build trust with new buyers.

Better Content Organization:

Structured the page with clear headings and sections (About Us, Products, Process, Certificates, Testimonials, Contact) to improve readability and reduce confusion for a global audience.
