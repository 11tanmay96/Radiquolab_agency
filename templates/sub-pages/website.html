Overview

Radiquolab Design Institute is a design education platform offering practical, industry-focused courses in UI/UX Design, AI, and related creative skills. The website is designed to help prospective students understand the institute's offerings, explore courses, evaluate credibility, learn about the learning journey, and ultimately take the next step toward enrollment. The experience focuses on communicating practical learning, career readiness, industry exposure, and student outcomes.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Web Development, Lead Generation Strategy
Problem Identification
High-Stakes Decision, Low Trust:

Prospective students investing in a course need strong proof of outcomes (placements, ratings, reviews) before committing — generic course pages don't build that confidence.

Complex Curriculum, Simple Decision Needed:

The course spans multiple skills (UI/UX, No-Code Development, Portfolio Building, AI Tools) that needed to be communicated clearly without overwhelming a first-time visitor.

Batch & Schedule Confusion:

With multiple batch timings (Morning, Weekend, Evening) and start dates, students needed an easy way to find and reserve a slot that fits their availability.

Pre-Enrollment Hesitation:

Many visitors have questions (fees, prerequisites, placement support, certification) that, if unanswered, cause drop-off before signup.

Solution

Conversion-First Hero with Instant Lead Capture:

Designed a hero section pairing the course value proposition with a "Get Free Demo Class" form (Name, Mobile, Course) and clear CTAs (Enroll Now / Download Syllabus) to capture leads at first glance.

Credibility Bar & Curriculum Breakdown:

Added a stats bar (1100+ Students Trained, 100% Placement Assistance, 5.0★ Rating, 8+ Years of Experience) followed by a 'Creative Courses That Make You Job-Ready' section detailing UI/UX Design, No-Code Development, and Portfolio Building tracks with AI-enhanced learning callouts.

Batch Scheduling with Direct Booking:

Built an "Upcoming Batches" section listing Morning, Weekend, and Evening batches with dates, timings, and duration, each paired with a "Reserve Your Seat" button to reduce scheduling friction.

Comprehensive FAQ Section:

Added top FAQ blocks — general course questions and "Questions Students Ask Before Enrolling" (salary expectations, career scope, prerequisites) — to proactively resolve objections and support SEO.
