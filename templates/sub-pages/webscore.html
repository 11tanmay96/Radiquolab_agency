Overview

FindWebScore is a free AI-powered website audit tool that lets users check their site's SEO, GEO, and AEO scores in seconds. The homepage is designed to communicate a technical, three-part scoring concept in a simple, non-intimidating way — driving instant, no-signup website analysis while educating visitors on why these scores matter.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Web Development, Content/UX Writing
Problem Identification
Unfamiliar Concepts (GEO & AEO):

While SEO is widely understood, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) are new terms most users wouldn't recognize — risking confusion right at the top of the funnel.

Friction Before Value:

Audit tools often require signup or account creation before showing results, which discourages first-time users from trying the product.

Low Trust in a New Category:

As an emerging tool in an unfamiliar space, the site needed to establish credibility quickly to convince users their analysis is accurate and worth acting on.

Explaining "Why" Before "How":

Users needed to understand why SEO, GEO, and AEO all matter together before they'd value a combined score, rather than just being handed a number.

Solution

Zero-Friction Hero with Instant CTA:

Designed a clear hero — "Check Your Website's SEO, GEO & AEO Score in Seconds" — with a single URL input field and "Analyze Website" button, reinforced by trust badges (100% Free, No Signup Required, AI-Powered Analysis, Instant Results) to remove all friction.

Credibility Stats Bar:

Added a stats section (250K+ Websites Analyzed, 98% Recommendation Accuracy, 15+ SEO Checks, 10+ GEO Checks, 8+ AEO Checks) right below the fold to build immediate trust with new visitors.

"Understanding the Three Scores" Education Section:

Created a dedicated explainer section breaking down What is SEO, What is GEO, and What is AEO into short, plain-language definitions with matching icons — turning unfamiliar acronyms into an easy learning moment.

Structured FAQ Section:

Built an accordion-style FAQ addressing definitional and practical questions (e.g., "What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?") to reinforce understanding and support organic search visibility.

Reinforced Closing CTA:

Closed the page with a bold gradient banner — "Improve Your Website's Visibility on Search Engines and AI Tools" — repeating the 250,000+ user trust signal and a secondary "Analyze Another Website" CTA to re-engage users after their first scan.
