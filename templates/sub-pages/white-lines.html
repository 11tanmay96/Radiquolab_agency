Overview

"Want To Enhance Your Office Design And Save Time In The Process?"

White Lines Studios is an interior design and architecture firm specializing in office and workspace transformation, built around the hook "Want To Enhance Your Office Design And Save Time In The Process?" The site walks visitors through the firm's expertise, work process, past case studies, team, and client testimonials to position it as an end-to-end partner for corporate and co-working space design.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Web Development, Content Strategy
Problem Identification
High-Investment Decision, Low Visible Proof:

Office redesigns are expensive, long-term commitments — clients need strong visual proof of past work before reaching out, but the firm's portfolio wasn't presented in a way that built immediate confidence.

Unclear Process, High Client Anxiety:

Business owners hesitate to commit to renovation projects when they don't know what's involved — timelines, disruption to operations, budget transparency — leading to drop-off before inquiry.

Multiple Service Lines, One Generic Pitch:

The firm offers distinct services (Corporate Office, Co-Working, Co-Living, Industrial Design), but without clear segmentation, visitors couldn't quickly identify if the firm suited their specific need.

People-Driven Business, Invisible Team:

In a relationship-driven industry like design, clients buy from people they trust — but the firm's team, leadership, and hands-on expertise had no dedicated presence to build that personal connection.

Solution

Case-Study-Led Portfolio:

Built a dedicated "Our Case Studies" section showcasing real completed projects (SESPL Techno, GC Bang and Associates) with photography and direct "View Work" links, letting prospects see tangible outcomes before booking a call.

Transparent 6-Step Work Process:

Designed a detailed process walkthrough – Understanding Your Vision → Space Planning → Material Selection & Budget → Execution & Project Management → Final Touches & Handover – to demystify the renovation journey and reduce client anxiety upfront.

Segmented Service Expertise:

Created an "Our Design Expertise" tab section (Corporate Office, Co-Working, Co-Living, Industrial Office) so different client types can immediately self-identify and explore relevant work.

Human-Centered Team & Leadership Section:

Added a "Driven by Design. Defined by People." section featuring founder and team photos with names, roles, and social links, plus director-level video testimonials – turning an anonymous firm into a team clients can trust.

Credibility Reinforcement Throughout:

Layered in recognizable client logos, a results stats bar (250+ Projects Completed, 200+ Happy Clients, 15+ Cities Covered), and consultation CTAs at every key decision point to move visitors from browsing to booking.
