Overview

MyDoc dashboard and patient record management UI

The "MyDoc dashboard and patient record management UI" project involved creating an intuitive web and mobile interface for doctors. The UI allows them to efficiently access and manage patient records, including appointments, insurance details, payment history, and patient notes. This streamlined system enhances the overall healthcare experience by providing doctors with a comprehensive and organized view of patient information.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Mobile App, Dashboard
Problem Identification
Difficulty Accessing Patient Information:

Doctors struggle to access comprehensive patient data quickly.

Inefficient Workflow:

Managing patient records is time-consuming and prone to errors.

Complex User Interface:

Existing UIs are cluttered and hard to navigate.

Solution

Centralized Patient Information Dashboard:

Developed a centralized dashboard to streamline access to appointments, medical history, insurance, and payment details.

Streamlined Workflow for Record Management:

Created an organized system that automates tasks like scheduling and record updates, boosting efficiency.

Simplified and Intuitive UI:

Built a simplified, intuitive UI with clearly organised sections for easy use.
