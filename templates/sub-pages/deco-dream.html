Overview

Interior design, without the guesswork.

Deco Dreams offers an interior design platform that uses artificial intelligence technology to help users design their living spaces. It provides features such as 3D visualization, room planning, and product suggestions to help users create personalized interior designs.

Services Utilized
UI/UX Design, Mobile App
Problem Identification
Lack of Visualization for Interior Design Ideas:

Users often struggle to visualize how different interior design styles would look in their own spaces.

Inability to Experiment with Design Themes:

Existing tools lack the ability to instantly transform a room’s design based on user preferences, limiting experimentation.

Limited User Interaction:

The lack of customization or room planning options leaves users without a true sense of how products fit into their space.

Solution

AI-Powered 3D Visualization:

Implemented AI-powered 3D visualization that allows users to upload an image of their room and instantly see how different design themes (e.g. modern, rustic) would appear in their specific space.

Dynamic Theme Selection:

Created a dynamic theme selection feature where users can easily switch between design styles and instantly view the results, providing a seamless experience for exploring multiple themes.

Interactive Room Planning Tools:

Integrated an interactive room planning tool that allows users to rearrange furniture, adjust layouts, and modify product placements within the 3D-rendered design, making them more involved in the process.
